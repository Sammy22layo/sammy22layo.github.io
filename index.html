About

I started out as a chemical engineer and ended up a data analyst, which turned out to be a shorter walk than it sounds — both are mostly about finding where a process leaks.

Today I work in insurance at Tangerine Africa in Lagos, where I build the reporting layer the business runs on: dashboards for production, retail and claims, and the Python and Power Automate jobs that keep them fed without anyone touching a spreadsheet. I sit on the transformation team as its analyst, which means I spend as much time mapping how a process actually works as I do querying it.

Outside work I build the engineering half end to end. The project below is a full platform — Azure Data Factory into Databricks, SCD2 dimensions, a delivery model, and the Power BI layer on top — built, tested and documented, including the parts that went wrong.

Two marketplaces, one set of numbers

A Brazilian e-commerce platform acquires a South African one. Finance needs one revenue figure that stops moving; operations need to know which platform is actually failing. An end-to-end platform — metadata-driven ingestion from 18 sources, a medallion warehouse with SCD2 merchant history, a delivery-promise model, and six Power BI pages served live.

Sorting waste from a phone camera

Recycling only works if waste gets separated, and most people guess. An image classifier that takes a photo — uploaded or shot on the spot — and names the material (cardboard, glass, metal, paper, plastic or trash) with a confidence score. Deployed on Streamlit, so anyone can open it and try their own photo.

~75% validation accuracy across six materials on 2,527 images — up from 64% once a scratch-built CNN that was overfitting gave way to transfer learning (MobileNetV2, ResNet50) with augmentation.
